# Market Gap — v0.1

## Observed market structure

The endurance friction-protection market already contains specialized products. Therefore the opportunity cannot be defined simply as "sports Vaseline."

Relevant solution types include:

- petroleum jelly
- anti-chafe sticks
- anti-chafe balms
- friction-defense products
- body-area-specific products
- tape / protective dressings

## Working market-gap hypothesis

The gap may be less about the absence of products and more about the absence of a coherent **scenario-based skin system**.

Athletes may select and combine products according to:

- duration
- distance
- body area
- weather
- sweat
- clothing/equipment
- personal tolerance for residue and feel
- need for reapplication

This hypothesis must be validated with structured user research.

## Strategic implication

The first brand should not promise to replace every existing solution. It should identify one high-value endurance scenario where a purpose-built product can deliver a clearly measurable improvement.

## Evidence discipline

Community reports are useful for generating hypotheses, but mixed user experiences should not be converted into universal claims. Competitor performance must be treated as an empirical question.
