# Competitor Landscape — v0.1

## Solution landscape

The current endurance friction-protection space includes several established solution types:

| Solution | Typical role | Strategic observation |
|---|---|---|
| Petroleum jelly | general barrier / friction reduction | familiar, inexpensive, widely adaptable |
| Anti-chafe sticks | convenient friction protection | purpose-built for active use |
| Anti-chafe balms | friction and moisture management | strong endurance-oriented use cases |
| Friction-defense products | body-surface protection | differentiated formats and use instructions |
| Tape / dressings | targeted mechanical protection | useful for specific body areas and failure modes |

## Named competitor set for future research

- Vaseline
- Body Glide
- Squirrel's Nut Butter
- 2Toms
- Gold Bond Friction Defense

## Strategic conclusion

The existence of specialized competitors means the opportunity should not be framed as simply inventing a sports version of petroleum jelly.

The more promising hypothesis is to organize product development around the athlete's scenario: duration, movement, environment, body area, and failure timing.

## Research caution

Competitor performance reports are mixed across athletes and conditions. User anecdotes should generate hypotheses, not be converted into universal claims.

Exact competitor specifications, claims, prices, market shares, and regulatory status should be maintained in dated research records before use in commercial planning.
