# Product Performance Requirements — v0.1

## Candidate requirement set

The first product may eventually need evaluation across:

- friction reduction
- persistence / duration
- sweat resistance
- water resistance
- heat stability
- repeated-motion durability
- skin compatibility
- residue / clothing transfer
- application ease
- reapplication ease

## Time-based test hypothesis

A possible research framework is to compare performance after increasing exposure periods, for example:

- initial state
- 1 h
- 2 h
- 4 h
- 6 h
- 8 h+

These time points are **hypotheses only**. They are not validated product standards and must not be presented as such until an appropriate test method and relevance to actual athlete use are established.

## GLIDE-SPEC 40 connection

Brand requirements should eventually be translated into machine-readable or structured technical targets within the main GLIDE-SPEC 40 development system.

The chain should be:

**Customer scenario → failure mode → product requirement → measurable test → formulation candidate → validation → user outcome**

## Important constraint

No numerical performance threshold should be fixed in this document until the relevant test method, baseline, variability, and consumer relevance are established.
