# First Product Concept — v0.1

## Working concept

**Long-Duration Skin Protection / Endurance Skin Protection**

The first product should solve a specific endurance scenario rather than attempt to become a universal sports cosmetic from day one.

## Functional role

Potential functions to investigate:

- reduce friction
- maintain a protective film over time
- tolerate sweat and moisture
- remain stable during repeated movement
- minimize unpleasant residue
- remain compatible with clothing and equipment
- allow practical initial application and reapplication
- maintain skin compatibility

These are development hypotheses, not established specifications.

## Category boundary

Anti-chafe is a key function but should not define the entire brand.

The product should establish the broader idea of **skin protection designed around endurance conditions**.

## Product-selection principle

Before choosing a formulation, identify:

1. the highest-value customer problem
2. the exact activity scenario
3. the failure mechanism
4. the measurable performance requirement
5. the acceptable sensory and usage tradeoffs

Only then should formulation work be optimized.

## Status

Concept stage. No final formula, claims, packaging, or regulatory positioning is established.
