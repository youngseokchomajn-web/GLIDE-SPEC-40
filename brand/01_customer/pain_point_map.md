# Runner Pain Point Map — v0.1

This is a research framework, not a completed evidence base.

## Body areas to investigate

- inner thighs
- groin
- buttocks / gluteal cleft
- nipples
- underarms
- sports-bra contact areas
- waistband / garment contact points
- feet / toes / shoe contact points

## Questions for every body area

1. What problem occurs?
2. At what distance or elapsed time does it begin?
3. Under what environmental conditions does it worsen?
4. What does the runner currently use?
5. Why is the current solution considered insufficient, inconvenient, or undesirable?
6. Would the runner pay more for an improved solution?
7. Can the problem realistically be addressed by a cosmetic formulation?
8. Is the need better solved by formulation, apparel, tape, equipment, behavior, or a combination?

## Cross-variable matrix

### Distance

- 10K
- Half marathon
- Marathon
- 50K
- 100K+

### Environment

- dry
- hot / humid
- rain / wet
- cold
- mixed conditions

### Existing solution

- petroleum jelly
- anti-chafe stick/balm
- friction-defense product
- tape / protective dressing
- combinations

### Failure timing

Capture the first detectable discomfort, first meaningful degradation, reapplication point, and end-of-activity condition.

## Strategic objective

Find the specific **moment or combination of conditions that existing solutions do not adequately solve for a meaningful group of endurance athletes**.

That finding should become the basis for the first commercial product requirement rather than assuming a formulation problem first.
