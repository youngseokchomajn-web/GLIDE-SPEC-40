# Brand Planning Changelog — v1.1 Addendum

Date: 2026-09-13

## v1.1 — Community-first concept gate and Korean scenario discrimination

### Strategic change

The project no longer requires a separate 5–10 person qualitative interview pilot solely to discover generic failure modes before the Korean study.

Existing community/public-web evidence is now treated as the discovery layer. Korean primary research is reserved for unresolved local/commercial discrimination.

### Added

- `brand/05_research/community_beachhead_analysis_v0.1.md`
- `brand/05_research/community_to_korea_gap_analysis_v0.1.md`
- `brand/05_research/korean_runner_survey_field_instrument_v0.2.md`

### Changed

Previous sequence:
`community discovery → 5–10 person interview pilot → larger Korean survey → concept selection`

New sequence:
`community/public-web evidence → beachhead gap analysis → targeted Korean scenario/commercial study → concept selection`

The Korean instrument is now organized around S1/S2/S3 discrimination, actual switching, economics, and an S4 escape hatch for unexpected scenarios. Generic discovery, broad brand awareness, ingredient preference, and premature format questions are downgraded.

### Decision impact

The Korean study is no longer intended to rediscover whether chafing, sweat, reapplication, clothing interaction, or non-topical substitutes exist. Those are already established as hypothesis-level signals in the community evidence.

The Korean study must answer:

> **Which specific Korean endurance-running failure scenario deserves to become the first commercial product?**

### Evidence boundary

Community evidence remains hypothesis/discovery evidence and does not establish prevalence, efficacy, superiority, WTP, or regulatory eligibility. Korean primary research remains a purposive decision sample unless representative sampling and weighting are separately designed.

### Downstream lock

No formula, ingredient, final format, duration threshold, superiority claim, package, or technical acceptance threshold is locked by this change.
