# Brand DNA — v0.1

## Working principles

### 1. Start from movement

The brand begins with what happens to skin when the body moves for a long time.

### 2. Design from conditions

Products should be designed from time, motion, environment, clothing, equipment, and body area rather than from conventional cosmetic categories alone.

### 3. Validate in context

A product intended for endurance should ultimately be evaluated under relevant endurance conditions, not only in ordinary cosmetic-use contexts.

### 4. Respect existing solutions

The brand does not need to attack Vaseline, Body Glide, Squirrel's Nut Butter, 2Toms, tape, or other existing solutions. The job is to solve a defined problem better and prove it.

### 5. Protect the athlete's attention

Application, feel, reapplication, residue, and packaging should reduce friction in the athlete's routine rather than create another task.

## Core conceptual variables

**TIME × MOTION × ENVIRONMENT**

## Long-term product system

**PROTECT → PERFORM → RECOVER → OPTIMIZE**

## Brand character hypothesis

- technical, but not clinical
- performance-oriented, but not aggressive
- Korean in formulation/R&D origin, but globally legible
- evidence-led, but understandable to athletes
- specialized, but expandable beyond running
