# Positioning — v0.1

## Working category

Endurance / Performance Skin Care

## Positioning hypothesis

For people who put their skin through prolonged movement and environmental stress, the brand develops skincare from the conditions of the activity itself, rather than adapting ordinary skincare to sport.

## Competitive frame

The initial market includes:

- petroleum jelly / general-purpose barrier products
- anti-chafe sticks and balms
- friction-defense products
- tape and protective dressings
- body-area-specific sports products

The strategic opportunity is not simply another anti-chafe formula. It is a coherent system organized around the athlete's scenario and timeline.

## Potential differentiation

### Product-centric market

Current solutions are commonly selected as individual products for a particular problem.

### Scenario-centric brand hypothesis

The brand can organize product development around:

**distance × duration × movement × environment × body area**

This should be validated through customer research rather than assumed as a market fact.

## Positioning guardrails

- Never rely on unsupported claims of competitor failure.
- Do not claim superior duration until tested.
- Do not equate sports skincare with medical treatment.
- Keep the brand broader than anti-chafe.

## Working message

> Built around the activity, not adapted to it.

Status: strategic working language, not a finalized slogan.
