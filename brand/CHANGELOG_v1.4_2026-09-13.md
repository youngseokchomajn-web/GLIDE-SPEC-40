# GLIDE-SPEC 40 Brand Changelog — v1.4 — 2026-09-13

## Purpose

Record the transition from broad public-web/community discovery to the first-product concept definition stage.

## Changed

### 1. First product concept advanced v0.2 → v0.3

Updated:

- `brand/03_product/first_product_concept.md`

Key change:

- S1 — long-duration running skin–garment interface protection is now the **leading first-product beachhead**.
- S2 — sports-bra / under-breast / waistband interface remains a differentiated adjacent scenario.
- S3 — maintenance/reapplication is treated as a cross-cutting UX requirement rather than a separate product category.
- The product is defined as an endurance skin-protection concept around a failure scenario, not as a "sports Vaseline" or "longer-lasting anti-chafe" product.

### 2. Research mode changed

Broad discovery is now considered converged at the current decision level.

The active sequence is:

`PUBLIC WEB / COMMUNITY SATURATION → SCENARIO DISCRIMINATION → BEACHHEAD DECISION → PRODUCT CONCEPT → TECHNICAL VALIDATION`

Further discovery should occur only when a specific falsification question can materially change the concept decision.

### 3. Decision lineage extended

Added:

- `D009 — Stop broad discovery and move to first-product concept definition`

The decision records why S1 survived the strongest apparel-only falsification question and why technical/formulation work remains downstream.

## Evidence interpretation boundary

This update does **not** establish:

- Korean prevalence
- market size
- willingness to pay
- objective product performance
- exact persistence/duration
- competitor superiority/inferiority
- final formula
- final format
- final packaging
- final price
- clinical/medical efficacy
- regulatory claim eligibility

Community/public-web evidence remains hypothesis and scenario evidence, not controlled efficacy evidence.

## Downstream implication

The next workstream is not another generic evidence collection pass. It is to translate S1 into **formulation-neutral product requirements**, then compare feasible formats/manufacturing routes and hand the resulting requirements into GLIDE-SPEC 40 technical validation.

## Change condition

Reopen the beachhead decision only if new evidence:

1. makes S2 or a new S4 materially stronger than S1;
2. shows S1 is predominantly solvable through apparel/fit alone;
3. reveals a materially different recurring failure mechanism; or
4. contradicts the current system-level model.
