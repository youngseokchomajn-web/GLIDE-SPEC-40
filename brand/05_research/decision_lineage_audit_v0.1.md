## 2. Decision lineage

### D001 — Reject "sports Vaseline" positioning

Observation → existing petroleum jelly and specialist products already have real users → generic superiority framing is strategically weak → anti-chafe becomes one function inside endurance skin.

Status: RECORDED in `decision_log.md`.

### D002 — Start with endurance runners

Observation → marathon/ultra combines long duration, repetitive motion, sweat/moisture, clothing/equipment and environmental exposure → concentrated high-consequence use environment → endurance runners become initial beachhead.

Status: RECORDED.

### D003 — TIME × MOTION × ENVIRONMENT

Observation → failure varies with elapsed time, movement and environment → use these as the initial abstraction while preserving body area, clothing and equipment as sub-variables.

Status: RECORDED.

### D004 — Community evidence is hypothesis-generating evidence, not truth

Observation → community reports are detailed but contradictory → contradiction can reveal segmentation and context → preserve individual cases and context rather than averaging them away.

Status: RECORDED in `decision_log.md` and `community_evidence_registry.md`.

### D005 — Search for the failure moment

Observation → generic "chafing" is too broad → commercial value lies in when/where/current-solution failure occurs → map body area × distance × time × environment × solution.

Status: RECORDED.

### D006 — Do not assume duration superiority

Observation → longevity reports conflict → exact persistence is not established → duration remains a test variable.

Status: RECORDED.

### D007 — Brand and technical GLIDE-SPEC 40 layers remain distinct

Observation → mixing market decisions with formulation/testing obscures provenance → keep brand work under `brand/` with downstream technical mapping.

Status: RECORDED.

### D008 — Do not lock the first product before identifying the beachhead failure scenario

Observation → several recurring scenarios exist → formula/format-first selection risks technical solution driving product definition → Long-Run Friction Shield remains provisional while the specific beachhead is unresolved.

Status: RECORDED in `decision_log.md`.

### Method reversal — Community replaces the discovery pilot

Original approach → 5–10 person discovery interview pilot.

New evidence/judgment → repeated community/public-web evidence already covers the generic failure-discovery layer sufficiently for the current gate.

Decision → community beachhead analysis replaces the mandatory discovery pilot. The old interview protocol remains preserved as superseded/hold rather than deleted.

Status: RECORDED.

### Method reversal — Global-first, Korea-localization

Original tendency → move quickly to Korean primary research.

New judgment → public web/community evidence should be exhausted before primary research; Korea research should only answer questions not adequately answered by global/public evidence.

Decision → global/public web becomes the saturation/discovery layer; Korean evidence is localization evidence, not a mandatory gate.

Status: RECORDED.

### Current correction — Korean survey is optional, not the next mandatory gate

The repository contains a Korean runner survey instrument and an execution plan. The instrument remains a reusable localization tool. The execution plan is explicitly superseded for the current research phase.

Current active rule:

> Continue web/community evidence collection until the beachhead decision can be made or the remaining uncertainty is demonstrably not answerable from public data.

A Korean survey should only be executed if a specific unresolved decision materially requires it.

## 3. Round 9 impact on the current concept

Round 9 does not overturn the current ranking.

### S1 — Leading

Recent global and Korean community evidence reinforces the cluster of:

`long duration × sweat/heat × inner thigh/groin × garment movement/migration × topical + clothing alternatives`

The important refinement is that S1 should not be described as merely a better lubricant. The evidence increasingly points toward **prolonged skin–garment interface protection under endurance conditions**.

### S2 — Principal alternative

Recent sports-bra/under-breast/band + equipment cases reinforce a distinct female interface cell and show that tape and garment changes can become substitutes when topical protection is insufficient.

### S3 — Cross-cutting variable

Reapplication remains operationally important, but recent cases show that reapplication can be a response to an underlying garment, sweat, anatomy, or duration failure. Therefore S3 should not automatically determine the product format.

### S4 — Open

Recent intergluteal and other anatomy-specific cases reinforce the need to keep unexpected scenarios eligible to overturn the current ranking.

## 4. What is already sufficiently answered by public/community evidence

1. The broad anti-chafe category is already populated.
2. Users employ both topical and non-topical solutions.
3. Failure depends on conditions, not mileage alone.
4. Sweat/water, heat/humidity, repeated movement, garment migration/seams and equipment interaction recur across cases.
5. Reapplication is a real operating variable.
6. Sensory burden, staining/transfer, odor and cleanup can influence retention or switching.
7. Female sports-bra/under-breast/band interfaces deserve separate treatment.
8. There is no single incumbent that wins universally across users and conditions.
9. Generic "anti-chafe for runners" is not sufficient differentiation by itself.
10. Recent Korean community evidence independently reproduces the broader system-level failure pattern, although it does not establish Korean prevalence.

These remain hypothesis-generating public/community findings, not objective efficacy claims or population prevalence estimates.

## 5. Questions still worth investigating on the web

Continue only where new evidence can change the concept decision:

- Which S1/S2/S3 failure patterns recur independently across communities?
- Are there S4 patterns with stronger intensity + recurrence + switching signals?
- What causes users to abandon topical products for clothing, tape or equipment changes?
- Which failures occur late in long-duration activity versus at initiation?
- Which sensory/cleanup problems repeatedly cause switching?
- Which product categories already solve each candidate scenario convincingly?
- Are there commercially defensible gaps after accounting for existing products and non-topical substitutes?
- What Korean/local evidence is available publicly that materially changes the global picture?

Do not collect web cases merely to increase case count. Prefer evidence that discriminates between competing scenarios or overturns a current assumption.

## 6. Deferred decisions

Until the beachhead scenario is selected, do not lock:

- exact anatomy as the permanent first SKU cell
- ingredient
- formula
- stick/balm/cream/gel format
- exact duration
- superiority claim
- clinical efficacy claim
- package architecture
- final price
- regulatory claim wording

Technical testing remains downstream of concept selection.

## 7. Record-integrity requirements going forward

For every material research pass:

1. preserve source/context;
2. assign stable evidence IDs where practical;
3. record positive and negative reports;
4. record contradictions explicitly;
5. separate observation from interpretation;
6. separate interpretation from decision;
7. record what would falsify/change the decision;
8. update the relevant versioned document;
9. add a changelog entry or addendum when strategy changes;
10. never silently replace an old decision; mark it superseded and preserve the prior rationale.

## 8. Audit conclusion

The project has a usable historical record for the major research and strategic decisions made so far. Round 9 strengthens the existing S1/S2/S3/S4 model without justifying a final beachhead selection.

The main remaining improvement is not more generic documentation; it is tighter evidence-to-decision provenance for future web passes and explicit recording of any decision reversal.

The active research mode is now:

`PUBLIC WEB / COMMUNITY SATURATION → SCENARIO DISCRIMINATION → BEACHHEAD DECISION → PRODUCT CONCEPT → TECHNICAL VALIDATION`

Korean primary research is optional and conditional, not an automatic next step.
