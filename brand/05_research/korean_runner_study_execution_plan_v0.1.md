# Korean Runner Study Execution Plan — v0.1

Date: 2026-09-13

> **STATUS: SUPERSEDED / NOT ACTIVE**
>
> This document records an earlier plan to execute a Korean runner scenario-discrimination study. It is retained for decision-history integrity. It must not be interpreted as the current next step.

## Why superseded

The project has adopted a **public-web/community-first** research rule.

The current objective is product-concept selection, not validation of a preselected product. Existing global/community evidence already covers much of the generic failure-discovery layer. Therefore a Korean primary study is not automatically required.

The active sequence is:

`PUBLIC WEB / COMMUNITY SATURATION → SCENARIO DISCRIMINATION → BEACHHEAD DECISION → PRODUCT CONCEPT → TECHNICAL VALIDATION`

Korean primary research may be used later only if a specific unresolved question materially affecting the decision cannot be answered adequately from public evidence.

## Historical purpose

This document originally converted `korean_runner_survey_field_instrument_v0.2.md` into a field-execution plan for discriminating S1/S2/S3 while preserving S4.

The historical plan included a preferred sample of 100–120 qualified Korean endurance runners and a minimum directional sample of 60–80. Those numbers are retained only as historical planning information and are **not a current recruitment instruction**.

## Historical decision question

> Among Korean endurance runners, which recurring skin-friction scenario has the strongest combination of problem intensity, recurrence, existing-solution gap, actual switching, economic relevance, solvability, and differentiation to become the first commercial product beachhead?

## Historical candidate scenarios

- S1: long-duration inner-thigh/groin + high sweat + garment motion
- S2: long-duration female sports-bra/under-breast/waistband interface
- S3: maintenance/reapplication/carry/reachability failure
- S4: unexpected recurring scenario

## What remains reusable

The field instrument can remain available as a **conditional localization tool**. If future public-web research identifies a decision that genuinely requires Korean primary evidence, the instrument can be reviewed and revised before use.

## Current rule

Do not execute this study merely because it was once planned.

Do not treat a Korean survey as a prerequisite for selecting the first product if public/community evidence becomes sufficiently discriminating.

If a primary study is later proposed, document the exact unresolved decision it is intended to resolve and why public evidence is insufficient before fielding.

## Version note

This status correction preserves the historical plan rather than deleting it. The active research rule is recorded separately in `decision_lineage_audit_v0.1.md` and must govern subsequent work.
