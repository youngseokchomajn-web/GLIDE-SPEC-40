# Concept Pilot Interview — v0.1

Date: 2026-09-13

## Purpose

Before launching the full Korean runner survey, conduct a small 5–10 person qualitative pilot to verify that the research instrument actually separates **high-value failure scenarios** rather than merely measuring general anti-chafe awareness or product preference.

This is a customer/problem discovery exercise. It is not a product efficacy test.

## Core question

> 어떤 러닝 상황에서 기존 피부 마찰 해결책이 더 이상 충분하지 않다고 느끼며, 그 순간 다른 해결책으로 바꿀 만큼 가치가 큰가?

## What the pilot must discover

1. The concrete failure moment: where, when, under what conditions.
2. The actual mechanism perceived by the runner: friction, sweat, garment movement, seam, equipment, migration, sensory burden, etc.
3. What solution was being used immediately before failure.
4. What the runner did next and whether that behavior actually changed the outcome.
5. Whether the problem is recurrent or a one-off event.
6. Whether the runner has already switched products or methods.
7. Whether switching was driven by performance, usability, sensory experience, price, or another factor.
8. Which scenario appears important enough to support a dedicated product concept.

## Recruitment

Target: 5–10 Korean runners.

Seek variation rather than statistical representativeness:
- 10K/half/marathon/ultra experience
- short vs long typical run duration
- road/trail
- male/female/relevant anatomy
- low/moderate/heavy sweating
- recurrent vs occasional chafing
- users of petroleum jelly, specialized anti-chafe, tape, clothing/equipment solutions, and no solution

Do not treat pilot percentages as population prevalence.

## Interview flow

### 1. Recent event reconstruction

Start with:

> 최근 장거리 러닝에서 피부가 불편했던 순간을 하나만 구체적으로 설명해 주세요.

Then reconstruct in chronological order:
- route/activity
- distance
- elapsed time
- weather/temperature/humidity
- sweat/water
- clothing
- equipment
- body area
- what was applied before the run
- what changed immediately before the problem appeared
- what happened next

Do not introduce product categories until the participant has described the event in their own words.

### 2. Failure moment

Ask:
- 처음 이상하다고 느낀 순간은 언제였나요?
- 그때 피부와 옷/장비 사이에서 무엇이 일어나고 있었다고 생각했나요?
- 기존에 바른 제품이 있었다면 무엇이 달라졌나요?
- 제품이 없어졌다고 느꼈나요, 아니면 옷/장비 움직임이 더 큰 문제였나요?
- 그 상태가 계속되면 러닝을 중단하거나 방법을 바꿀 정도였나요?

### 3. Current workaround

Ask:
- 그 상황을 해결하기 위해 실제로 무엇을 했나요?
- 다음 러닝에서는 무엇을 바꿨나요?
- 제품을 바꿨나요?
- 테이프, 옷, 장비 조절 등 비화장품 해결책을 사용했나요?
- 아무것도 바꾸지 않았다면 왜 기존 방법을 계속 사용했나요?

### 4. Switching trigger

Ask:
- 지금 사용하는 해결책을 바꾸게 만들 수 있는 상황은 무엇인가요?
- 실제로 바꿔본 적이 있다면 무엇에서 무엇으로 바꿨나요?
- 가장 결정적인 이유는 무엇이었나요?

Probe only when relevant:
- protection failure
- reapplication
- sweat/water
- heat/cold
- greasy/sticky feel
- staining/transfer
- odor
- skin irritation
- application/reachability
- portability
- price/value

### 5. Concept reaction

Only after the participant has described their own problem, show a neutral concept:

> 장시간 운동에서 피부와 의류·장비가 반복적으로 접촉하는 상황을 고려해 설계하는 피부 보호 화장품.
> 구체적인 지속시간이나 우월성은 아직 정하지 않은 초기 제품 컨셉입니다.

Ask:
- 이 설명이 본인의 문제와 연결되나요?
- 어떤 상황에서는 유용할 것 같나요?
- 어떤 상황에서는 전혀 필요 없을 것 같나요?
- 현재 사용하는 방법보다 바꿀 이유가 생기려면 무엇이 달라야 하나요?

Do not reveal a preferred format or ingredient.

## Coding framework

Every meaningful event should be coded across:

`USER SEGMENT × BODY AREA × TIME × MOTION × ENVIRONMENT × CLOTHING/EQUIPMENT × CURRENT SOLUTION × FAILURE MOMENT × RESPONSE × SWITCHING TRIGGER`

### Primary scenario codes

- F1 time/persistence failure
- F2 sweat/water failure
- F3 garment migration
- F4 seam/edge focal friction
- F5 equipment/pack interaction
- F6 product migration
- F7 reapplication burden
- F8 reachability/application burden
- F9 sensory/cleanup burden
- F10 transfer/staining
- F11 skin compatibility concern
- F12 clothing/equipment solution dominates topical solution

Multiple codes are allowed.

## Scenario quality score

This is a qualitative prioritization tool, not a statistical score.

For each scenario record:

- Frequency across pilot participants: observed count only
- Recurrence: one-off / occasional / recurrent
- Severity: low / moderate / high
- Behavioral consequence: none / adjustment / product switch / run disruption
- Existing-solution dissatisfaction: low / moderate / high
- Switching evidence: none / stated willingness / actual switch
- Specificity: broad / condition-specific / highly specific
- Strategic fit with Endurance Skin concept: low / medium / high

Do not calculate a market-size claim from these scores.

## Contradiction handling

Preserve contradictory reports.

Examples:
- One runner may find petrolatum highly effective while another finds it fails under heavy sweat.
- One runner may dislike reapplication while another considers it normal.
- One body area may be solved by topical protection while another is better solved by tape or garment adjustment.

Contradictions should be investigated by comparing conditions rather than averaged away.

## Pilot decision gates

### P0 — Instrument comprehension
Can participants understand the questions without being led toward a product category?

### P1 — Failure reconstruction
Can interviews reliably recover a concrete event rather than generic opinions?

### P2 — Scenario separation
Can we distinguish different failure mechanisms and contexts?

### P3 — Switching signal
Can we identify actual behavior change, not just stated preference?

### P4 — Concept discrimination
Does the neutral Endurance Skin concept resonate strongly with a specific scenario, or does it remain too broad?

### P5 — Full survey readiness
Proceed to the larger field survey only if the instrument produces sufficiently specific, interpretable scenarios.

## Changes allowed after pilot

The following may be revised before the full survey:
- question wording
- response options
- failure taxonomy
- segment definitions
- concept card wording
- concept trade-off attributes
- interview probes

Do not revise the underlying product concept simply to make pilot results fit it.

## Output

Create a pilot synthesis containing:

1. individual case summaries
2. scenario matrix
3. recurring failure moments
4. contradictions and condition differences
5. actual switching examples
6. non-topical substitutes
7. candidate high-value beachhead scenarios
8. unresolved questions for quantitative survey
9. recommended changes to field instrument
10. explicit decision: proceed / revise / stop

## Evidence boundary

Pilot interviews do not establish prevalence, efficacy, superiority, clinical benefit, regulatory eligibility, or national market size.
