# Beachhead Decision Readiness — v0.1

Date: 2026-09-13

## Purpose

Record whether the public-web/community evidence base is sufficiently converged to stop broad discovery and move into formal first-product concept definition.

This is a decision-readiness document, not a final product specification.

## Active research rule

The project has intentionally moved away from automatic Korean primary research. Public web/community evidence is the default discovery and saturation layer.

Primary research is only justified if a specific unresolved decision cannot be answered from public evidence and materially changes the product choice.

## Decision question

> Is there enough evidence to define the first product around a specific endurance-running failure situation without prematurely deciding formula, ingredient, format, exact duration, superiority, package or price?

## Evidence convergence

### Finding 1 — The broad category is already occupied

Anti-chafe products, compression apparel, liners, tapes and fit changes all compete to solve friction-related problems. Therefore the opportunity cannot be defined as generic anti-chafe.

### Finding 2 — Failure is system-dependent

Across public/community evidence, outcomes vary with:

`SKIN × PRODUCT × GARMENT × EQUIPMENT × MOTION × SWEAT/WATER × TIME`

This is the most stable cross-pass finding.

### Finding 3 — Long-duration running is a strong context

Long runs, marathon-distance and ultra-distance reports repeatedly expose failures that may not appear in shorter activity. However, no fixed mileage or hour threshold should be claimed.

### Finding 4 — Apparel is both competitor and complement

Good fit, compression, seam design and coverage can solve some cases. Other cases persist despite apparel changes or require topical protection in addition to apparel. Therefore the first product must be designed to work within the user's total solution system.

### Finding 5 — S1 survives falsification

S1 = long-duration inner-thigh/groin skin protection under high sweat + garment motion.

The strongest attempted falsification was the hypothesis that S1 is simply an apparel problem. Current evidence does not support that reduction. Topical and apparel solutions repeatedly coexist, and failures can persist after apparel changes.

### Finding 6 — S2 remains strategically valuable

S2 = long-duration sports-bra/under-breast/waistband interface under heat/humidity.

S2 has strong differentiation potential and clear system-level evidence, but current public evidence does not justify replacing S1 as the initial beachhead.

### Finding 7 — S3 is not the concept

Reapplication, portability and reachability are important operating requirements, but they appear to be downstream responses to changing conditions rather than the highest-level customer problem.

## Current ranking

1. **S1 — Leading first-product beachhead**
2. **S2 — Principal differentiated alternative / expansion cell**
3. **S3 — Cross-cutting UX and operation requirement**
4. **S4 — Open only if new evidence materially overturns the current model**

## Decision readiness

**HIGH.**

The evidence is sufficient to move from broad discovery into formal concept definition.

This does NOT mean:

- S1 has been clinically validated;
- a formula has been selected;
- a format has been selected;
- a duration claim has been proven;
- superiority has been proven;
- Korean prevalence or WTP has been established.

## Recommended next decision

Proceed to a formal **first-product concept v0.3** centered on the S1 situation, while preserving S2 as an explicit adjacent scenario and preserving the system-level category architecture.

The concept should be phrased around the use situation and failure mechanism, not around a specific ingredient or format.

### Working concept statement

> **An endurance skin-protection product designed for long-duration running, where repeated motion, sweat/moisture and garment contact can make ordinary skin-protection routines insufficient or inconvenient.**

### Category architecture

**Endurance Skin / Performance Skin**

### Design lens

`TIME × MOTION × ENVIRONMENT`

with the expanded validation matrix retained for later technical work:

`BODY AREA × TIME × MOTION × TEMPERATURE × HUMIDITY/WATER × CLOTHING/EQUIPMENT × APPLICATION FORMAT × SKIN COMPATIBILITY`

## Explicit non-decisions

Do not lock at this stage:

- exact anatomical cell as the permanent SKU definition;
- ingredient;
- formula;
- stick/balm/cream/gel;
- exact persistence duration;
- superiority claim;
- clinical claim;
- package architecture;
- final retail price;
- regulatory claim wording.

## Stop rule for further discovery

Broad web/community discovery should stop unless new evidence can do one of the following:

1. demonstrate that S2 or S4 is materially stronger than S1 on problem intensity + recurrence + switching + commercial defensibility;
2. demonstrate that S1 is primarily solvable by apparel/fit and therefore lacks a product-level opportunity;
3. reveal a materially different recurring endurance-skin failure mechanism that changes the first-product architecture;
4. reveal a major contradiction to the current system model.

Otherwise, additional case collection is diminishing-return documentation rather than decision-making.

## Lineage

This readiness assessment is based on the cumulative public-web/community passes, community beachhead analysis, evidence-gap analysis, and the Round 11 falsification pass.

It supersedes neither prior research nor prior decisions; it records the current state after those passes.
