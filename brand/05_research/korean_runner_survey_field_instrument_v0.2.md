# Korean Runner Survey Field Instrument — v0.2

Date: 2026-09-13

## Purpose

This version changes the Korean primary study from broad problem discovery to **scenario discrimination**.

The community/public-web evidence has already established the main failure-system hypotheses. The Korean study must therefore determine which specific Korean endurance-running scenario deserves to become the first commercial beachhead.

Primary hypotheses:

- **S1:** Long-duration inner-thigh/groin protection under high sweat + garment motion
- **S2:** Long-duration sports-bra/under-breast/waistband interface under heat/humidity
- **S3:** Long-duration protection where initial application works but maintenance/reapplication becomes the failure
- **S4:** Unexpected scenario emerging from respondent evidence

This is customer/problem and market-behavior research. It is not an efficacy, clinical, duration, superiority, or regulatory study.

## 1. Screener and profile

- Yes / No → terminate

**S2.** Longest distance completed in the last 12 months:

## 4. Scenario classification

Code each respondent into the best-fitting scenario based on the reconstructed event. Do not tell the respondent the codes.

### S1 — Long-duration inner-thigh/groin + high sweat + garment motion
Conditions may include sweat/wetness, repeated movement, seam/garment migration, and recurring protection failure.

### S2 — Long-duration female clothing-interface problem
Sports-bra, under-breast, band/strap, waistband or related high-shear interface under heat/humidity or movement.

### S3 — Maintenance/reapplication failure
Initial protection is acceptable, but maintenance, reapplication, reachability, carryability or changing conditions become the meaningful failure.

### S4 — Other
A recurrent scenario not adequately represented by S1–S3. Preserve the original wording and do not force-fit it.

## 6. Scenario value

For the respondent's own scenario, rate 1–5:

- Problem intensity
- Frequency/recurrence
- Dissatisfaction with current solution
- Disruption to running
- Difficulty of current workaround
- Importance of solving it

**Q17.** If this problem were meaningfully improved, how likely would you be to change your current solution?
- Definitely not / probably not / unsure / probably / definitely

This is stated intent and must be reported separately from actual switching.

## 7. Economics

**Q20.** Annual additional spending specifically to solve this problem:

- approximate KRW amount

**Q21.** At what price would a new solution feel:

## 8. Neutral scenario concept choice

Show the same neutral descriptions to all qualified respondents after Sections 2–7.

**S1 card:**
> 장거리 러닝에서 허벅지·사타구니 주변의 반복적인 피부-의류 접촉과 땀/습기 상황을 고려해 설계하는 피부 보호 제품.

**S2 card:**
> 장거리 러닝에서 스포츠브라·언더브스트·밴드 등 여성 의류와 피부가 반복적으로 접촉하는 상황을 고려해 설계하는 피부 보호 제품.

**S3 card:**
> 장거리 러닝에서 시간이 지나며 재도포·휴대·도달성·환경 변화가 문제가 되는 상황을 고려해 설계하는 피부 보호 제품.

**S4 card:**
> 위 설명에 포함되지 않는 본인의 반복적인 장거리 러닝 피부 문제를 위한 제품.

**C1.** Which concept is most relevant to your own problem?
- S1 / S2 / S3 / S4 / None — keep current solution

**C2.** Which would you be most likely to consider buying?
- S1 / S2 / S3 / S4 / None — keep current solution

**C3.** Why? Open text.

Do not state exact duration, superiority, ingredient, clinical outcome, or final format.

## 9. Concept discrimination rule

Do not select a beachhead from concept-choice percentage alone.

Compare each scenario on:

`PROBLEM INTENSITY × RECURRENCE × EXISTING-SOLUTION GAP × ACTUAL SWITCHING × ECONOMIC RELEVANCE × PRODUCT SOLVABILITY × DIFFERENTIATION`

Preserve contradictory cases.

A high stated-interest score without actual switching or economic relevance is insufficient for SELECT.

## 10. What has been removed/downgraded from v0.1

- Generic "does chafing exist?" discovery is no longer a major survey objective.
- Generic brand-awareness questions are not a primary research priority.
- Broad stick-vs-cream preference is deferred until a scenario is selected.
- Detailed ingredient preferences are removed from the concept gate.
- Exact duration/performance questions are excluded from the customer survey.
- The former 5–10 person interview pilot is not required before fielding solely to discover failure modes; community evidence serves that discovery role.

## 11. Minimum reporting outputs

1. S1/S2/S3/S4 assignment by respondent
2. problem intensity and recurrence by scenario
3. actual switching by scenario
4. current solution and substitute mix
5. reapplication/carry/reachability burden
6. spend and price sensitivity
7. neutral concept choice including keep-current option
8. contradiction cases and explanations
9. segment cells that change the scenario ranking
10. explicit SELECT / CONDITIONAL SELECT / HOLD / REJECT recommendation

## 12. Evidence boundary

This survey can establish customer/problem, behavior, segmentation and commercial signals within the recruited sample.

It does not establish:
- national prevalence from a convenience sample
- objective friction reduction
- exact persistence duration
- superiority
- clinical prevention/treatment
- final formula or ingredient
- final format
- regulatory eligibility

## Version delta

v0.2 was created because community/public-web evidence has already saturated the generic failure-discovery layer. The survey is now optimized for **S1/S2/S3 discrimination, actual switching, economics, and an S4 escape hatch**.
