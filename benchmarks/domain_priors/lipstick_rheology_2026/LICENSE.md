1. **Non-Commercial Research & Prior Benchmark Isolation:**

2. **Strict Production Separation:**  
   The final GLIDE-SPEC 40 production model (M4) is calibrated and qualified **strictly on internal proprietary pilot run data (`DataOrigin.REAL_PILOT`)**. No copyrighted third-party raw files are directly packaged or sold as proprietary assets.
