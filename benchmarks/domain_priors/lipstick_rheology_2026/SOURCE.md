# Scientific Source & Experimental Metadata

## 1. Primary Publication
- **Title:** Lipstick structure revealed by multimodal strain- and time-dependent rheology
- **Authors:** A. Gautier, K. To, R. Botte, M. Dupire, T. Rouxel, F. Artzner
- **Journal:** *Soft Matter*, 2026 (Royal Society of Chemistry)
- **DOI:** [10.1039/D5SM01032B](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5SM01032B)
- **Article Link:** https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2026/sm/d5sm01032b
- **Supplementary Info:** ESI ZIP (26.988 MB), containing raw data for all figures.

## 2. Experimental Setup & Test Specimens
- **Samples Analyzed:** Three commercial lipstick formulations (designated Lip-A, Lip-B, Lip-C) spanning diverse structural wax-oil-filler-silicone networks.
- **Formulation Components:** Waxes (candelilla, carnauba, microcrystalline, ozokerite, synthetic), liquid emollients (castor oil, polybutene, esters), silicone derivatives, mineral fillers, organic/inorganic pigments.
- **Instruments:**
  - Stress-controlled rotational rheometer (Anton Paar MCR 302 / 502)
  - Cross-hatched parallel plates (sandblasted or profiled geometry, diameter 20 mm / 25 mm, gap 0.5 ~ 1.0 mm) to eliminate wall slip.
  - Optical birefringence microscopy (LC-PolScope) for crystal morphology during in-situ controlled thermal quenching.
- **Testing Modes:**
  - SAOS: Strain amplitude sweep ($\gamma = 0.001\% \sim 100\%$, $\omega = 1\,\text{Hz}$)
  - Frequency sweep: $\omega = 0.01 \sim 100\,\text{rad/s}$ in linear viscoelastic regime (LVR)
  - Temperature sweeps: $20^\circ\text{C} \rightarrow 90^\circ\text{C} \rightarrow 20^\circ\text{C}$
  - Transient: Creep compliance $J(t)$ under constant shear stress, stress relaxation $G(t)$ after step strain $\gamma_0$
  - Nonlinear: Large Amplitude Oscillatory Shear (LAOS) with Lissajous-Bowditch curve decomposition
  - Cooling rates evaluated: $0.1^\circ\text{C/min}$ (near-equilibrium annealing), $1.0^\circ\text{C/min}$ (standard cooling), $10.0^\circ\text{C/min}$ (rapid mold quench).
