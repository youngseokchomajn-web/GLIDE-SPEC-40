# GS-40 Coordinate Feature Mapping

## 1. Formulation Wax System Mapping
- In Huynh et al. (2020), total wax is strictly fixed at **17.0 wt%**, which matches the exact total wax phase of GLIDE-SPEC 40 Rev.7.3.
- Candelilla substitution (L4 vs L1) reveals that removing Candelilla and substituting with lower-melting ester waxes:
  - Decreases bending hardness ($4.85\,\text{N} \rightarrow 3.20\,\text{N}$, $-34\%$)
  - Increases needle penetration firmness ($165\,\text{gf} \rightarrow 188\,\text{gf}$, $+14\%$)
  - Decreases skin pay-off ($14\,\text{mg} \rightarrow 10\,\text{mg}$, $-28\%$)
  - Reduces kinetic friction ($0.28 \rightarrow 0.24$, enhanced glide)

## 2. Aging Kinetics Feature Mapping
- **Thermal Degradation Rate (25°C vs 45°C):**
  - $\Delta H_{\text{aging}}(12\text{W}, 25^\circ\text{C}) \approx -2.7\%$ (negligible drift for stable control)
  - $\Delta H_{\text{aging}}(12\text{W}, 45^\circ\text{C}) \approx -34.0\%$ (thermal softening index)
- **Syneresis / Sweating Boundary:**
  - L2 (alkenones substituting ozokerite) sweated at 45°C Week 8 due to insufficient microcrystalline/branched hydrocarbon oil-trapping matrix.
  - In GS-40, **MQ Resin (12% active) + Silica Dimethyl Silylate + Synthetic Wax** serves as the anti-sweating structural trap, preventing oil bleeding even at 45°C.
