# Source & Protocol Metadata (Huynh et al. 2020)

## Primary Citation
- **Title:** Evaluation of alkenones, a renewably sourced, plant-derived wax as a structuring agent for lipsticks
- **Authors:** H. Huynh, R. Sharma, G. W. O’Neil, M. Chandler, G. B. Dudek, G. B. Kasting, et al.
- **Journal:** *International Journal of Cosmetic Science*, 2020, 42(3), 292–302.
- **DOI:** [10.1111/ics.12597](https://doi.org/10.1111/ics.12597) / [PMC9291794](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9291794/)

## Experimental Matrix ($N = 384$)
- **Formulations:**
  - L1 (Control, no alkenones): 3.5% Microcrystalline + 3.5% Ozokerite + 7.0% Candelilla + 3.0% Carnauba (Total Wax: 17.0%)
  - L2 (Ozokerite substitute): 3.5% Alkenones + 3.5% Microcrystalline + 7.0% Candelilla + 3.0% Carnauba (Total Wax: 17.0%)
  - L3 (Microcrystalline substitute): 3.5% Alkenones + 3.5% Ozokerite + 7.0% Candelilla + 3.0% Carnauba (Total Wax: 17.0%)
  - L4 (Candelilla substitute): 7.0% Alkenones + 3.5% Microcrystalline + 3.5% Ozokerite + 3.0% Carnauba (Total Wax: 17.0%)
- **Aging Protocol:**
  - Temperatures: 25°C (Room Temperature) and 45°C (Accelerated Thermal Cabinet)
  - Timepoints: Day 1, Week 4, Week 8, Week 12
  - Replicates: $N = 12$ stick specimens per condition to enable destructive testing
  - Total specimens: $4 \times 2 \times 4 \times 12 = 384$ physical sticks.
