- **Journal:** *Gels*, 2021, 7(3), 97
- **DOI:** [10.3390/gels7030097](https://doi.org/10.3390/gels7030097)
- **License:** CC BY 4.0

## Rheological Test Protocol (From Supplementary Material)
- **Instrument:** TA Instruments AR 1000 Rheometer
- **Geometry:** 8 mm parallel plate
- **Gap:** 0.7 mm
- **Ramp Protocol:** Temperature ramp from 20°C to 150°C (heating) and 150°C to 20°C (cooling) at 1°C/min
- **Frequency:** 1 Hz (6.28 rad/s)
- **Strain:** 1.0% (within linear viscoelastic regime)
- **Measured Parameters:**
  - $G'$ (storage modulus, Pa)
  - $G''$ (loss modulus, Pa)
  - $\tan\delta$ (loss factor)
  - Crossover point $G' = G''$ ($\tan\delta = 1.0$) defines the structural gel-to-sol phase transition temperature.
