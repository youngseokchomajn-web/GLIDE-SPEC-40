### GLIDE-SPEC 40 Usage Boundary:
Within GLIDE-SPEC 40, this dataset is restricted to the **Layer 0 Domain Prior & Benchmark** layer. It is used exclusively to evaluate thermal crossover mechanics and rheological stability priors without serving as empirical calibration data for physical GS-40 batches.
